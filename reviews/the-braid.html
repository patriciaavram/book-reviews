The Braid

by Laetitia Colombani

Rating: ★★★★☆

Summary

In this unforgettable international bestseller, three women from very different circumstances around the world find their lives intertwined by a single object and discover what connects us—across cultures, across backgrounds, and across borders. In India, Smita is an untouchable. Desperate to give her daughter an education, she takes her child and flees her small village with nothing but resourcefulness, eventually heading to a temple where she will experience a rebirth. In Sicily, Giulia works in her father's wig workshop, the last of its kind in Palermo. She washes, bleaches, and dyes the hair provided by the city's hairdressers, which is now in short supply. But when her father is the victim of a serious accident, she discovers that the company's financial situation is dire. Now she must find a way to save her family's livelihood. In Canada, Sarah is a successful lawyer and twice-divorced mother of three children whose identity is wrapped up in her work. Just as she expects a big promotion, her life is shattered when she's diagnosed with cancer. A moving novel of hope and renewal, The Braid is a celebration of womanhood and the power of connection and perseverance.

My Thoughts

This is a story about how women are all intertwined regardless of where we are located physically. It is a beautifully written novel, showing how three very different environments can create extreme resilience in three very different women. Smita's story is deeply tragic from the start, being born into extreme poverty in India is a circumstance which I cannot even fathom. She has learned from the society in which she grew up, and now is determined to give her daughter a better life. She is headstrong, stubborn, and ambitious, and will stop at nothing to provide her daughter with a better life than her own. Giulia's story is a softer one, albeit still very sad. Working in her family's business, she is suddenly faced with one of the toughest challenges that you have to go through in your life, which is losing one of your parents. She develops a connection with a man in the meantime, while struggling to process her emotions, and when she finds the secret behind his father's accident, she must pull through and come up with a plan to save her family from bankrupcy. And she does, executing to success an idea coming from her lover, and bringing a new age of success to her family's business. Last but not least, Sarah receives a life-altering cancer diagnosis, and has to battle with her jealous coworkers, looking for any opportunity or weakness to push into a career opportunity. All these women have to come to terms with an extreme life change, and all three of these women successfully rise to the mental, physical and emotional challenges that come their way. Beautifully written novel, beautiful stories, and beautiful message.
