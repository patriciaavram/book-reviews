Pick A Color

by Souvankhham Thammavongsa

Rating: ★★★★☆

Summary

Ning is a retired boxer, but to the customers who visit her nail salon, she is just another worker named Susan. On this summer's day, much like any other, the Susans buff and clip and polish and tweeze. They listen and smile and nod. But beneath this superficial veneer, Ning is a woman of rigorous intellect and profound complexity. A woman enthralled by the intricacy and rhythms of her work, but also haunted by memories of paths not taken and opportunities lost. A woman navigating the complex power dynamics among her fellow Susans, whose greatest fears and desires lie just behind the gossip they exchange. As the day's work grinds on, the friction between Ning's two identities—as anonymous manicurist and brilliant observer of her own circumstances—will gather electric and crackling force, and at last demand a reckoning with the way the world of privilege looks at a woman like Ning.

Pick A Color - Goodreads

My Thoughts

I read this book in one sitting, and I really liked it. I thought the plot was very original, and the characters were also very interesting. I loved getting to see this sort of sisterhood bond form between all the Susans throughout the day. Overall it was a pleasant read but it wasn't a 5 stars because it lacked a certain something within the plot. I found it relaxing to read about Ning's day-to-day life but other than that there wasn't a lot that was revealed to us about her life, which I think might've been the intent of the author. One thing I will mention though is that seeing how Ning pictures the world, including her clients, was really interesting to read about. It really reminds me that everyone has a different perspective when they look at anything. Beauty is in the eye of the beholder but so is judgement.
