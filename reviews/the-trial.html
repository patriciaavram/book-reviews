The Trial

by Franz Kafka

Rating: ★★☆☆☆

Summary

Written in 1914 but not published until 1925, a year after Kafka's death, The Trial is the terrifying tale of Josef K., a respectable bank officer who is suddenly and inexplicably arrested and must defend himself against a charge about which he can get no information. Whether read as an existential tale, a parable, or a prophecy of the excesses of modern bureaucracy wedded to the madness of totalitarianism, The Trial has resonated with chilling truth for generations of readers.

The Trial - Goodreads

My Thoughts

Maybe Kafka is a little far over my head, because I just could not get hooked on this story at all. I didn't care about Josef K., and I didn't care about his trial. It felt tedious to follow this novel at times, through all the twists and turns that the bureaucracy was taking K. through. This story felt like a first draft, something unfinished and unedited, a run-on story basically, with no end in sight. I might simply not be prepared to handle the deeper picture of such a novel. Will need to revisit this in the future, but overall a very bland read.
