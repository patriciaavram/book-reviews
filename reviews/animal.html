My Thoughts

Where do I even start describing this book. We follow along with a girl named Joan's life, following a tragic event that changed her perspective on life and motivated her move to Los Angeles to find her estranged sister. I found this novel to be beautifully written. Lisa Taddeo has a very specific writing style that follows a train of thought but does not restrict itself to finishing discussing a single subject at once. This is written in a very intuitive, human way, combining and stringing along different stories when she thinks of them, and I found that quite endearing.

However, there are a multitude of things about this novel that I disliked. First of all, the violent depictions of sex and rape were unecessary to the story. What could have been an interesting depiction of the cycle of trauma just ended up being descriptions of violent scenes, with no visible reason to include them in the story. To me, the way that the main character is written does not resemble any real woman that I have ever known, in the sense that Joan is such an incredibly male-centric character, where her entire life revolves around men. Again, to me this makes her very unlikeable, hard to relate to and uninteresting. I understand that her previous trauma definitely caused some of this behaviour but this is simply not something that I've ever encountered in real life, which made it difficult to empathise with her and quite honestly, made the story unlikeable. I honestly didn't really understand the message that this book was trying to portray, or even the point of the story in general. It felt like we were simply following along with Joan's escapism to Los Angeles, and going with life along with her. I have to say though, her unlikely friendship with the daughter of the woman whose husband had an affair with Joan was very unpredictable, but quite a welcome subplot. It added a layer of color to the story, showing how two women who have been wronged by men all their lives can bond very deeply. Unfortunately, this book was not for me.
